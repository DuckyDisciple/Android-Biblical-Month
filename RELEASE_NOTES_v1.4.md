**Release date:** September 4, 2026  
**Version:** 1.4 (versionCode 10)

## Highlights since v1.3

- **Personal schedule** – Tap a day to add, edit, or delete your own events
- **Device calendar overlay** – Optionally show events from calendars you choose in Settings
- **Location that sticks** – GPS or city search; no more sticky “New Jersey” from the default fallback
- **Ghost event cleanup** – Hide in BibliCal only, or remove from your phone calendar (with permission)
- **Today’s Schedule** – See today’s personal and phone events on the Today screen
